# Requirement to test traceability

Generated by `scripts/check_traceability.py` from `safety/hazards.yaml`
and the tests themselves. Do not edit by hand.

Every requirement traces up to a safety goal and a hazard, and down to at
least one test that verifies it. The build fails on a gap in either
direction: a requirement nobody tests is a hole in the argument, and a test
claiming a requirement that does not exist inflates the matrix while
verifying nothing that was asked for.

## Hazards

| | Hazard | Harm |
|---|---|---|
| HAZ-01 | Conveyor moves while a person is inside the guarded area | Entanglement or crush injury at the belt or transfer points |
| HAZ-02 | The cell resumes motion after a stop without anyone asking it to | Injury to a person who entered believing the machine was stopped |
| HAZ-03 | The capper actuates while a hand is at the station | Crush or pinch injury |
| HAZ-04 | The controller commands the machine using a view of the plant that is no longer true | Any of the above, plus damage, because every interlock the program applies is computed from inputs that stopped being real |
| HAZ-05 | A defective bottle leaves the cell | Product safety. An unfilled or uncapped bottle reaching a consumer |

## Safety goals

| | Goal | From |
|---|---|---|
| SG-01 | No actuator shall be energised while torque is unavailable | HAZ-01, HAZ-03 |
| SG-02 | Motion shall resume only after a deliberate human action, and never as a side effect of a condition clearing | HAZ-02 |
| SG-03 | The controller shall not command motion on plant data it cannot confirm is current | HAZ-04 |
| SG-04 | The cell shall be still in every state except Execute | HAZ-01, HAZ-03 |
| SG-05 | A product failing inspection shall be ejected before it can leave | HAZ-05 |
